### Q1
A company paid 500,000 for a machine and 20,000 to the contractor who installed
it. Scrap from the crating was sold for 5,000.

- [ ] 500,000
- [x] 515,000
- [ ] 520,000
- [ ] 525,000

> **Right:** 515,000 - installation is part of getting the asset ready to use, and the scrap recovery reduces its cost.
> **500,000** - ignores both adjustments. Tempting if you stop reading at the invoice.
> **520,000** - adds installation but forgets the recovery. The most common miss.
> **525,000** - adds the recovery instead of subtracting it. A sign error.

### Q2
Which cost is an operating expense rather than part of the asset?

- [x] The first year's insurance policy on the machine
- [ ] Freight paid to deliver the machine
- [ ] Wages of the contractor who installed it

> **Right:** insurance covers running the asset, not getting it ready to use.
> **Freight paid to deliver the machine** - delivery is part of getting it in place, so it is capitalized.
> **Wages of the contractor who installed it** - installation labour readies the asset, so it is capitalized too.
